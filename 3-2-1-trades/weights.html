How do you weigh trade values?

This site uses a combination of methods to determine the fairness of a trade. Note that these do not take into account the demand of a specific pet. This site was made with the intention of looking at baseline/objective rarities first and then having demand be something considered afterward.
This is to ensure the end result is as fair as possible when used.

This tool does not current include horror's list pets.

3 year rule

Pets within a 3 year range are equal to one another. This means that the year before and the year after a particular year are all equal.
This does NOT mean that all pets are equal regardless of year. Only pets within neighboring ranges are.

  • 2026 = 2025
  • 2025 = 2026 AND 2025 = 2024
  • 2024 = 2025 AND 2024 = 2023
  • 2023 = 2024 AND 2023 = 2022
  • 2022 = 2023 AND 2022 = 2021
  • 2021 = 2022 AND 2021 = 2020

So on and so on...

This does NOT include pets dated from 2011 or older. Due to historic trade data any pet from 2011 or older is weighed differently.

2:1 and 3:1 conversions

This will only apply to pets whose rarity is rare or lower. For pets very rare or higher, they will rely on external factors/other weights.

c$ prices

  • Pets below common: multiple for 1 c$
  • Commons:
    • 2018-2026: 0.5-1 c$
    • 2012-2017: 1 c$
    • 2011-2009: 1-2 c$
  • Uncommons:
    • 2018-2026: 1 c$
    • 2012-2017: 1-2 c$
    • 2011-2009: 2-3 c$
  • Very Uncommons:
    • 2018-2026: 2-3 c$
    • 2012-2017: 3-4 c$
    • 2011-2009: 4-5 c$
  • Extremely Uncommons:
    • 2018-2026: 3-5 c$
    • 2012-2017: 4-7 c$
    • 2011-2009: 6-9 c$
  • Rare:
    • 2025-2026: 6-8 c$
    • 2022-2024: 8-10 c$
    • 2018-2021: 10-15 c$
    • 2015-2017: 15-20 c$
    • 2014-2011: 18-24 c$
    • 2010-2011: 25-35 c$
    • 2008-2009: 40-50 c$
